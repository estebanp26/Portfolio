About

I build pipelines that don't phone home.

I'm a junior developer focused on applied AI for document processing. The work I care about is the unglamorous kind: parsing a PDF, extracting what matters, deciding whether a model should be trusted with the answer, and shipping the result somewhere a person can act on it.

My main project runs OCR and a small language model entirely on my own machine through Ollama. Attachments never leave the box. When confidence is low, the system escalates to a human instead of guessing — which is the part I'd argue matters most in document automation.

I work on Arch Linux and Hyprland, I document what I build as I go, and I'm currently moving from building pipelines to understanding the data coming out of them.

Operations

Docker · pytest · 115 commits

▸Local-first. Attachments are processed on-machine. Works offline with Ollama and degrades to a deterministic fallback if the model isn't up.
▸Separation of concerns enforced. n8n does no OCR and no inference — it consumes the engine over HTTP. The engine handles no identity management.
▸Guardrails before AI. Deterministic rules and a confidence threshold decide what reaches the model; low-confidence cases go to manual review.
▸Tested. Nine pytest suites covering RBAC, security, performance indexes, and workflow simulation.
